## How this committee writes

1. Read the sentence out loud. If it stumbles, rewrite.
2. Name the product in the first line of the close: availability, not speed.
3. Proof before slogan. PaySecure years and the growth cycles are the proof. Do not invent the number of years or the names of the cycles.
4. Template language may remain where André Silva has not specified. Commercial language that will go to the team may not.

## This pass (19 Sep 2026, two niches)

Clients have international traffic. Market enablement and orchestration. We do not take their merchants and we do not dispute their accounts.

Never write "we do not sell to merchants" as a blanket. Never write "marketing enablement": the glossary term is market enablement.
