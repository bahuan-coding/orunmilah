## Two niches, no conflict of interest

Clients have international traffic. We do market enablement and orchestration for them. We do not take their merchants and we do not dispute their accounts.

Local merchants sit in each geography where we have structure. The Website Factory digitises them. They use our capture channels as a local player, on the cheap commercial policy of that geography, because of our aggregated volume. They do not access the same markets as the large foreigners our clients bring.
