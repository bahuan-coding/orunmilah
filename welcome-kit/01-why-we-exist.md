# 01 · Why We Exist

Status: Settled

## What we were hired to do

PaySecure brings the flow. We create the connectivity that flow needs in Latin America. That is the whole agreement, and it is why this company exists: PaySecure is the demand side, SambaPay is the supply side, and what we supply is not a connection. It is a local commercial policy.

Market enablement is the service. It is how we serve PaySecure, and it is what every merchant that arrives through them is actually buying.

## Why a local commercial policy is the product

In most countries in this region, more payment traffic runs on local methods than on the big global brands. A business in Europe or the United States that wants to sell here does not need another gateway. It needs local prices, local approval and local rules: the same conditions a local merchant gets.

Getting them means a regulated local entity, PCI compliance, contracts with local acquirers and banks, and someone who keeps every one of those relationships alive, every day. That is a hard strategic decision, and it is not the expertise of the people who have to make it. Most of the market sells connectivity and then leaves the client alone with the local entities. We keep the relationship.

## Who this is for

Businesses that live on volume with thin margins, where a small change in rate changes the whole result. High-volume merchants. Merchants with an expansion plan and no local presence. Cross-border sellers and online-service players who never entered a market because the local commercial policy was out of reach.

For them two numbers decide everything: approval and rate. Approval times margin is what we move.

## Why it matters

Margin mechanics in these markets have not changed in a long time and will not change soon. What can change is who gets access. Without a local commercial policy, cross-border businesses are never truly included in the markets they want to serve. That exclusion is the problem we exist to remove.

## Our mission

Grow the capacity of our clients' checkouts into markets they cannot reach alone. Local rails, local prices, the best approval we can get, through local entities that are regulated and PCI compliant, with every interface to those entities handled by us, so the client's executive team stays on its own priorities.

We spread the load on purpose: several payment methods, instant payments, cards and local instruments, several acquirers side by side. Risk is limited by design, not by luck.

We are a paytech: a fintech specialised in delivering payments into our clients' accounts. That is the whole job.
