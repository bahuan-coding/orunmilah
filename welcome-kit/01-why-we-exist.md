# 01 · Why We Exist

Status: Settled

## The problem

Selling across borders is easy to imagine and hard to do. In most countries, most payments run on local methods, not on the big global brands. A merchant from Europe or the United States who wants to sell in Brazil, Mexico or Colombia needs local acquiring, local payment methods and, above all, a local commercial policy: local prices, local approval rates, local rules.

Getting that is a hard strategic decision. It needs a regulated local entity, PCI compliance, contracts with local acquirers and banks, and someone who keeps every one of those relationships alive. The solutions on the market connect you, then leave you alone with the local entities. Most executive teams do not have this expertise, and should not need to.

## Who suffers most

Businesses that run on volume with thin margins. For them, a small change in rate changes the whole result. High-volume merchants. Merchants with an expansion plan and no local presence. Online-service players and cross-border sellers who never entered a market because the local commercial policy was out of reach.

## Why it matters

Market mechanics around margin have not changed in a long time and will not change soon. What can change is who has access. Without a local commercial policy, cross-border businesses are never truly included in the markets they want to serve. That exclusion is the problem we exist to remove.

## Our mission

Give merchants with thin margins access to local payment rails, at local prices, with the best approval possible, through fully regulated, PCI-compliant local entities, and take care of every interface with those entities so the merchant's team can focus on its own business.

We are a paytech: a fintech specialised in delivering payments into our clients' accounts. That is the whole job.
