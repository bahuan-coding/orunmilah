## What SambaPay is

SambaPay (sambapay.tech) is the rebrand of A55 Payments and part of PaySecure (paysecure.net), a global payments platform. We are PaySecure's market enabler in Latin America: PaySecure brings the volume, we open the local rails. We report to London.
