# 05 · How We Work

Status: In discussion

## Seven principles

1. **Everyone knows the direction.** If you cannot say in one sentence where the company is going, the fault is ours, not yours. The Map is always current.
2. **Clear roles.** Every one of us has a title and owns named processes, so a colleague in London, an acquirer or a merchant knows exactly who runs what. One exception: André Silva uses his name only. Rank does not decide; the process owner does.
3. **Speed is the product.** Our obsession is answering every interface need between a client and a local entity faster than anyone. Inside the company, the same: answer today.
4. **Reconciliation first.** The day starts with the right number. Nothing else is trusted until the book ties.
5. **Open is honest.** What is undecided is marked Open, in writing, for everyone. We do not hide gaps; we close them.
6. **Nothing beyond the one thing.** If a task does not connect a merchant to local rails or protect that connection, we question it.
7. **Diligent from day zero.** Every merchant is screened before any acquirer sees it. Every acquirer's checklist is complete before go-live. Speed never buys an exception.

## Decisions

- Anyone may propose. The owner of the area decides. André Silva decides whenever it touches money held, a merchant stopped, an acquirer relationship, or London.
- A decision that changes direction is written in the decision log with the options, the choice and the reason. One line is enough.
- Ambiguity: choose the best-founded option, write the assumption down, move. Escalate only what is irreversible or what only André Silva can decide.

## Communication

- Written first, in English, in the shared groups. One group per topic, not per person. Who to call is in 06; the groups are listed in the Company OS, people.
- Bad news travels fastest. A blocker is reported the hour it appears, not at the weekly.
- With London: facts, numbers, dates, asks. No adjectives.
- With acquirers, Visa, Mastercard and regulators: Sheila writes; André Silva signs holds and offboards. Nobody else speaks for the company to them.
